Iced strawberry matcha

Makes 1 glass · 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp (3–4 g) ceremonial matcha
  • 60 ml hot water (about 80 °C)
  • 5 fresh strawberries
  • 1–2 tbsp maple syrup or honey
  • 180 ml cold milk (or oat milk)
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Mash the strawberries with the syrup until jammy.
  2. Spoon the strawberry mix into the bottom of a tall glass and fill with ice.
  3. Pour the cold milk over the ice.
  4. Sift the matcha into a bowl, add the hot water and whisk in a zigzag until frothy.
  5. Slowly pour the matcha on top for the layered look.
  6. Stir before drinking and enjoy.
